# Milk & Meadow

**Small-batch ice cream, made from local dairy.**

Milk & Meadow is the website of a neighborhood ice cream parlor. It lets visitors explore our flavors, see the parlor, and book our ice cream cart for their next event.

## What We Offer

- **Handcrafted scoops.** Every batch is made from scratch with milk from local pasture-raised cows.
- **Seasonal flavors.** The menu follows the seasons, from classics like Vanilla Bean to specialties like Blue Moo Cookie Monster.
- **Our parlor.** A cozy neighborhood spot open daily, 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
- **The ice cream cart.** We bring the scoops to weddings, birthdays, school events and company gatherings.

## What You Can Do on the Site

### Home
- Meet the parlor with our story and what makes our ice cream different.
- Browse the signature flavors and tap any flavor for its description, price and dietary tags (such as gluten-free, vegan or dairy-free).
- Open the **full menu**, grouped by Classic, Chocolate, Fruity and Specialty, with prices.
- Jump straight to booking from any flavor with **Request for my cart**.

### Gallery
- Look through photos of our shop, cones and flavors.
- Tap a photo to enlarge it, then move through the photos with the arrows or your keyboard.

### Book a Cart
Send an inquiry to have our cart at your event. Tell us:

- Your name, email and phone number
- Event type (birthday, wedding, corporate, community event or other)
- Date, start time and venue
- Number of guests
- A favorite flavor and any special requests

Each booking includes the cart and scooper for up to 3 hours, three flavors of your choice, and cups, cones and toppings.

After you submit, you get a summary with a reference number, and we confirm by email within one business day.

## Booking Rules

- Book at least **3 days ahead** so we have time to prepare. Past dates can't be selected.
- Bookings are open up to **2 years ahead**.
- The cart serves **10 to 500 guests**.
- We have one cart, so only **one booking per date**.
